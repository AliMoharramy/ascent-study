# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit and the enforcement ideas of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(licensed CC BY 4.0). The summary below is how it applies here.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. This is a study tool: many users are students under
exam pressure, so we also value patience and kindness around mistakes and questions.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive; assume good faith.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Stay on topic and keep discussions in public where possible.
- Respect privacy: never post other people's personal data, secrets or exam-related private material.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or slurs, personal attacks, trolling, doxxing.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban a contributor. Instances of unacceptable behaviour can be reported privately to the
maintainers by using GitHub's **"Report content"** feature, or by contacting the maintainer through the contact details on
their GitHub profile. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the
project in public.
